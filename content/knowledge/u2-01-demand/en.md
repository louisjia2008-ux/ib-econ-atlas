Willingness requires purchasing power.  hold income and other conditions constant.  a price change moves the buyer along the same demand curve.

### Original scenario and reasoning

Illustrative example: coffee falls from 6 to 4 per cup and weekly purchases rise from two to four. Quantity demanded rises. With income and tastes unchanged, this is a movement along the curve, not a rightward shift.

Wanting a good without the ability to pay does not constitute effective demand.

Distinguish movements along demand from shifts and state the ceteris paribus assumptions.
