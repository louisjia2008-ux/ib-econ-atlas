### Original scenario and reasoning

Illustrative example: training aligned with actual vacancies may reduce mismatch, whereas courses without employer demand may not increase employment. Education spending can raise current demand, but supports long-run supply only if skills and productivity improve, often after a long lag.

Spending amounts are not sufficient evidence of productivity gains.

Explain productivity and potential-output channels, distinguish short-run AD from long-run AS and evaluate opportunity costs.
