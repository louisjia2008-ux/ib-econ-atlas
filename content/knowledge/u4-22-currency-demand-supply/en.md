### Original scenario and reasoning

Hypothetical scenario: holding risk and expectations constant, a higher relative home interest rate attracts asset purchases and can appreciate the currency. A rising risk premium may offset this.

The interest-rate link is conditional; speculation, inflation expectations and capital restrictions also affect flows.
