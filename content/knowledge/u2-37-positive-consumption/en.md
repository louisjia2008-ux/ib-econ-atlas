Consumers compare private benefit and price.  third-party gains are absent from their choice.  market consumption is below the social optimum.

### Original scenario and reasoning

Illustrative example: vaccination may reduce transmission risk and benefit others. Draw MSB above MPB. Subsidies, public provision and information can increase uptake, but capacity, trust and the size of external benefit determine effectiveness.

Education or healthcare does not automatically become a pure public good because it has social value.
