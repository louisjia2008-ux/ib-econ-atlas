Buyers and sellers plan at a common price.  quantities demanded and supplied coincide.  the market clears without guaranteeing equity.

### Original scenario and reasoning

Illustrative example: at a price of 8 buyers demand 60 units and sellers supply 60. This is an equilibrium pair. Equilibrium is a comparison benchmark; contracts, inventories and information delays can slow actual adjustment.

Equilibrium does not mean all needs are met or that the outcome is socially optimal.

Identify the changed determinant before comparing prices, quantities and stakeholders.
