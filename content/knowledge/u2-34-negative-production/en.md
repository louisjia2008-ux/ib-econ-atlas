Production damages third parties.  firms do not bear the full cost.  private equilibrium output exceeds the social optimum.

### Original scenario and reasoning

Illustrative example: factory discharge raises downstream water-treatment costs. Draw MSC above MPC, assume MSB=MPB and locate Q* where MSC meets MSB. Between Q* and Qm, social costs exceed benefits.

Showing pollution as MSB below MPB confuses production and consumption externalities.

Identify production/consumption and positive/negative effects, and label private equilibrium and the social optimum.
