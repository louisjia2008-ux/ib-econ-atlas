Increase purchases or disposable income.  initial spending creates income and further consumption.  aggregate demand shifts right and may narrow a recessionary gap.

### Original scenario and reasoning

Illustrative example: repairs to public facilities directly raise purchases, whereas only the spent portion of household support generates current demand. Output may respond more with idle resources; imports, saving and implementation delays weaken domestic effects.

Transfers are not government final purchases in national accounting.

Distinguish purchases/transfers and flows/stocks; evaluate leakages, lags, crowding out and debt.
