---
title: Sustainability
takeaway: A sustainable economic choice meets present needs while preserving
  resources and ecological capacity for people in the future.
definition: Sustainability is the ability of economic activity to continue over
  time without irreversibly weakening natural systems, social foundations, or
  the future capacity for production and well-being.
causalChain:
  - Production and consumption use natural and social resources
  - Some resources deplete and some pollution accumulates
  - Present choices alter the future feasible set
  - Policy and innovation try to incorporate long-run costs
realWorldExample:
  title: Responsible consumption and production
  sourceUrl: https://sdgs.un.org/goals/goal12
  observedAt: 2026-09-29
  explanation: United Nations Sustainable Development Goal 12 tracks resource
    efficiency, waste, and sustainable production, linking current output to
    material use and future environmental capacity.
misconceptions:
  - Sustainability does not require an end to all growth; it questions the
    resource and environmental path of that growth.
  - Technology can relax a constraint without automatically removing rebound
    effects or distributional problems.
examApplication:
  - Separate a short-run benefit from a long-run external cost.
  - Evaluate environmental effectiveness, transition costs, and how burdens
    differ across groups.
summary:
  - Present choices alter future production possibilities.
  - Finite resources and ecological capacity impose long-run constraints.
  - Sustainability brings intertemporal costs into decisions.
---
Economic activity relies on energy, materials, ecosystems, and social trust. If use exceeds regeneration or pollution exceeds the environment's capacity to absorb it, today's output can reduce future productive capacity and well-being.

Sustainability is an intertemporal test. Policy compares benefits and costs across generations. Resource prices, regulation, public investment, and innovation can redirect the path, but their effect ultimately depends on behaviour and institutional enforcement.
