### Original scenario and reasoning

Illustrative example: education support for low-income households can improve opportunity and productive capacity. Means testing improves targeting but may exclude households without records. Gradual withdrawal can reduce high effective marginal tax rates from abrupt benefit loss.

Redistribution includes services such as education and healthcare, not just cash.

State the equity criterion and compare efficiency, opportunity, incentives and targeting.
