Sales generate revenue.  deduct payments and opportunity costs.  the remainder is economic profit or loss.

### Original scenario and reasoning

Illustrative example: revenue 200, explicit costs 140 and implicit costs 40 yield accounting profit 60 and economic profit 20. Revenue of 180 gives zero economic profit while covering all opportunity costs. Normal profit is not zero accounting income.

A monopolist's MR can lie below AR because the lower price may apply to all units sold.

Separate firm and market diagrams, identify MR=MC output and read price from demand.
