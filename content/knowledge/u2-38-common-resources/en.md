### Original scenario and reasoning

Illustrative example: a fisher earns from an extra catch while reducing future fish stocks available to everyone. Quotas, enforceable rights or community management can limit extraction. Calling the resource non-rival would miss the central mechanism.

The tragedy is not an unavoidable law of nature; effective community rules can change incentives.

Identify production/consumption and positive/negative effects, and label private equilibrium and the social optimum.
