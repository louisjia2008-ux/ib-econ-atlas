### Original scenario and reasoning

Illustrative example: a merger may reduce competition but save logistics costs. Assess whether savings reach consumers, whether entry is feasible and whether coordination becomes easier. Splitting a natural monopoly may duplicate fixed networks, so remedies should reflect the market.

More firms is not a sufficient condition for improved efficiency.
