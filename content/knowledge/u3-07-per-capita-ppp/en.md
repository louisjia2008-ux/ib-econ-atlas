Aggregate growth may accompany population growth.  divide by population to examine averages.  PPP adjusts purchasing power across countries.

### Original scenario and reasoning

Illustrative example: real GDP grows 4% and population 2%, so per-capita growth is about 2%, precisely 1.04/1.02−1≈1.96%. PPP comparisons still do not reveal distribution, service quality or environmental differences.

A per-capita value is an average, not the income each person actually receives.

State indicators, formulas, units and limits; distinguish nominal, real, aggregate and per-capita measures.
