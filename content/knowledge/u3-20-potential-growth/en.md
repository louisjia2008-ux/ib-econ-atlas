### Original scenario and reasoning

Illustrative example: a more reliable electricity grid reduces downtime, lowering unit costs and expanding long-run capacity. More potential does not ensure immediate full utilisation; weak demand may leave actual output below the new potential.

Actual growth may merely close a negative gap rather than shift LRAS right.
