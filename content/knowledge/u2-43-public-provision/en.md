### Original scenario and reasoning

Illustrative example: a city pays for street lighting from taxes while a private contractor installs it. Public financing can coexist with private production. Evaluate residents' benefits, opportunity cost, competitive tendering and maintenance accountability.

Underprovision supports a case for intervention but does not prove every government project is worthwhile.

Classify rivalry and excludability and explain financing rather than merely stating government provision.
