---
title: Actual and potential growth
takeaway: Movement toward an existing PPC is growth in actual output, while an
  outward shift is growth in productive potential; the two can occur at
  different times.
definition: Actual growth is an increase in current output within existing
  capacity. Growth in production possibilities is an outward shift of the
  maximum feasible boundary caused by more or better resources or improved
  technology.
causalChain:
  - The economy may initially operate inside its frontier
  - Higher utilisation moves actual output toward the line
  - Investment, education, resource discovery, or technology expands capacity
  - The PPC shifts outward and permits greater long-run output
realWorldExample:
  title: A growth rate alone cannot separate recovery from capacity expansion
  sourceUrl: https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/NY.GDP.MKTP.KD.ZG
  observedAt: 2026-09-29
  explanation: World Bank real GDP growth data record changes in output. Growth
    after recession may mainly reuse idle factors, whereas sustained investment
    and productivity improvement are more likely to expand the long-run
    frontier.
misconceptions:
  - A rise in actual GDP does not by itself show that the PPC has shifted
    outward.
  - An outward shift can be biased toward one good rather than parallel.
examApplication:
  - Use movement of a point for actual growth and movement of the curve for
    potential growth.
  - Connect changes in resources, physical or human capital, and technology to
    the direction of the shift.
summary:
  - Movement from inside toward the frontier raises utilisation.
  - An outward shift expands maximum capacity.
  - The source of growth determines whether the shift favours one axis.
---
When an economy operates below full capacity, output can rise through higher employment and capital utilisation without changing the PPC. This pattern often describes a recovery.

A larger or more skilled workforce, capital accumulation, resource discovery, or technological progress expands the feasible set and shifts the PPC outward. Innovation confined to one sector can create a rotation or an uneven shift rather than a parallel movement.
