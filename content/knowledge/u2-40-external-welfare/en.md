Identify market and optimal quantities.  compare MSC and MSB on misallocated units.  sum the gaps rather than treating tax revenue as the loss.

### Original scenario and reasoning

Illustrative example: Q*=80, Qm=100 and MSC−MSB is six at Qm. If the gap grows linearly from zero, loss is ½×20×6=60. An optimal corrective tax equals marginal external cost at Q*, not mechanically the gap at Qm.

The triangle's height must be the relevant social cost-benefit gap, not an arbitrary market price.

Identify production/consumption and positive/negative effects, and label private equilibrium and the social optimum.
