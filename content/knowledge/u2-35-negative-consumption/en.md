The consumer receives private benefit.  consumption harms others.  social marginal benefit lies below private benefit and consumption is excessive.

### Original scenario and reasoning

Illustrative example: loud night-time music disturbs neighbours. MSB lies below MPB; without production externalities MSC=MPC. Harm to the consumer's own health is not an external cost, although it may involve imperfect information and demerit goods.

Distinguish harm to oneself from harm to third parties.

Identify production/consumption and positive/negative effects, and label private equilibrium and the social optimum.
