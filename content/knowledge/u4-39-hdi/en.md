Normalise dimensions.  combine schooling indicators into education.  the geometric mean limits full compensation of a weak dimension by a strong one.

### Original scenario and reasoning

Hypothetical scenario: two economies have similar income but one has better longevity and education, potentially giving it higher HDI. National averages still hide gender and regional gaps.

HDI does not add raw units directly and is not a comprehensive ecological or political-freedom measure.

State dimensions, units, years and average-value limits; compare using multiple indicators rather than rankings alone.
