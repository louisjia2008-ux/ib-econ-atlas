Fix a common price.  add each consumer's quantity demanded.  repeat at other prices to construct market demand.

### Original scenario and reasoning

Illustrative example: at a price of 5, A buys three units and B buys seven, so market quantity demanded is ten. At a price of 8 they may buy one and two, yielding three. Add quantities, never prices.

Market demand is neither average individual demand nor a vertical sum of prices.

Distinguish movements along demand from shifts and state the ceteris paribus assumptions.
