A lower price is needed to expand sales.  MR is below price.  at the profit maximum P normally exceeds MC and output is below the allocatively efficient level.

### Original scenario and reasoning

Illustrative example: MR=MC at Q=40, where demand gives price 12 and MC is seven. Price is 12, not seven. Monopoly does not guarantee profit: compare ATC. Innovation and scale economies can change cost comparisons.

A monopolist cannot choose price and quantity independently; demand still constrains it.

Separate firm and market diagrams, identify MR=MC output and read price from demand.
