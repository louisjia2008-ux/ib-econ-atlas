### Original scenario and reasoning

Illustrative example: automation raises output but removes some jobs. Retraining, transferable skills and mobility affect how widely gains are shared. Cleaner technology lowers emissions per unit, but output expansion may offset part of the environmental improvement.

Growth, development and sustainability are not identical concepts.
