Write amount units.  cancel units in conversion.  calculate change as new minus old divided by old.

### Original scenario and reasoning

Hypothetical scenario: at eight home units per dollar, 240 home units buy thirty dollars; at ten, they buy twenty-four. The home currency's dollar value falls 20%, not 25%.

An import bill can also change with quantity and foreign price; hold these conditions constant.

State the quotation and regime first, label demand/supply shifts, and distinguish price, quantity and lag effects.
