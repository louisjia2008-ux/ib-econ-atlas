Production creates spillovers such as knowledge.  firms cannot capture all social gains.  the market allocates too few resources.

### Original scenario and reasoning

Illustrative example: a firm's research generates knowledge that lowers other firms' costs. Draw MSC below MPC and use the same MSB to locate social and private output. Patents or research subsidies can strengthen incentives, but market-power costs and targeting matter.

The external benefit is an unpriced gain to third parties, not the firm's own sales revenue.

Identify production/consumption and positive/negative effects, and label private equilibrium and the social optimum.
