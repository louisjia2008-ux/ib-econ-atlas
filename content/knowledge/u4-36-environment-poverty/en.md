### Original scenario and reasoning

Hypothetical scenario: a village without reliable power relies on fuelwood. A logging ban alone may harm poor households; community management and clean energy improve incentives and alternatives together.

High consumption among wealthy groups can also damage the environment; responsibility cannot be assigned solely to poor people.
