### Original scenario and reasoning

Illustrative example: missing transport and digital access restrict a child's training and future employment. This mechanism does not reduce outcomes to personal motivation. Policy should identify credit, information, service-supply and discrimination barriers.

Observed income differences cannot be attributed to one cause from correlation alone.

Distinguish income, wealth, poverty and inequality; evaluate targeting, incentives and equity criteria.
