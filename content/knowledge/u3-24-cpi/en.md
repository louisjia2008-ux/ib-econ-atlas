### Original scenario and reasoning

Illustrative example: CPI rises from 120 to 126, so inflation is 5%, not 6%. Households with different food and energy weights experience different changes. Substitution, new products and quality changes also limit a fixed basket.

The index-point increase is not the same as the percentage increase.

Define and measure objectives before analysing causes, stakeholders and conflicts.
