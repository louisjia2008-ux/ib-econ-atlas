Estimate sustainable potential output.  compare actual with potential.  identify spare resources or excess-demand pressure.

### Original scenario and reasoning

Illustrative example: potential GDP=1000 and actual GDP=950 give a gap of about -5%. Output can grow from 930 to 950 and still have a negative gap. Potential is unobservable, so estimation error affects policy choices.

A negative gap does not require negative current growth.
