Hold inputs comparable.  compare output per unit of resources.  identify the productivity advantage.

### Original scenario and reasoning

Hypothetical scenario: A produces eight cloth units per hour and B five. A has absolute advantage in cloth, but another good is needed to establish comparative advantage.

Absolute advantage compares productivity; comparative advantage compares opportunity cost.

State small-country, homogeneous-good and cost assumptions; compare welfare and distribution. For HL calculations, start with opportunity costs.
