Fiscal expansion raises financing or resource demand.  interest rates or input costs may rise.  some private investment, consumption or net exports are offset.

### Original scenario and reasoning

Illustrative example: at capacity, public works compete for construction workers and raise private project costs. Such pressure may be weaker in a deep recession with monetary accommodation. Transport investment may also lower firms' costs and crowd investment in over time.

Higher government borrowing alone does not prove an equal fall in private investment.

Distinguish purchases/transfers and flows/stocks; evaluate leakages, lags, crowding out and debt.
