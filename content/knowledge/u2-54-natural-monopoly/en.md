### Original scenario and reasoning

Illustrative example: duplicating water pipes can waste resources. P=MC pricing improves allocative efficiency, but MC<ATC in a declining-cost range can create losses requiring subsidy. P=ATC recovers cost without necessarily achieving allocative efficiency.

Natural monopoly describes a cost structure, not every state-owned or large firm.

Separate firm and market diagrams, identify MR=MC output and read price from demand.
