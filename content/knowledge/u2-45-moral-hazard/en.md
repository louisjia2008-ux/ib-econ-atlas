A contract shares risk.  actions cannot be fully monitored.  a party may take more risk or less precaution.

### Original scenario and reasoning

Illustrative example: full insurance may reduce a driver's precautions against theft. A deductible shares losses and monitoring can reduce risk, but an excessive deductible weakens protection. Incentives are not the only evaluation criterion.

Moral hazard describes incentives, not a moral judgement about the person.
