Identify the output gap and objective.  examine design, approval, implementation and leakages.  weigh short-run stabilisation against long-run fiscal and supply outcomes.

### Original scenario and reasoning

Illustrative example: cash support may stabilise demand sooner, whereas rail construction has long preparation but may raise future capacity. High import propensity reduces domestic demand effects, yet imported equipment can expand supply. Financing currency, interest and future tax bases all matter for debt risk.

Neither deficit size nor a single multiplier establishes policy success.

Distinguish purchases/transfers and flows/stocks; evaluate leakages, lags, crowding out and debt.
