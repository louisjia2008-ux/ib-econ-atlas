### Original scenario and reasoning

Illustrative example: weak demand alongside skills mismatch can justify short-run demand support with long-run training; training alone may not immediately create jobs. In an overheated economy, stimulating demand may worsen inflation before new capacity arrives, so sequencing and implementation matter.

There is no context-free ranking of universally best policies.
