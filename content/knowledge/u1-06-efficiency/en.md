---
title: Efficiency
takeaway: Efficiency avoids waste and directs scarce resources toward valued
  uses, but an efficient outcome is not automatically equitable.
definition: Economic efficiency describes a situation in which resources are not
  wasted and, as far as possible, are allocated to uses that create greater
  value or satisfy more highly valued wants; productive and allocative
  efficiency are commonly distinguished.
causalChain:
  - Identify scarce inputs and feasible outputs
  - Remove idle capacity and unnecessary input loss
  - Move production to the feasible frontier
  - Ask whether the output mix reflects social preferences
realWorldExample:
  title: Improving energy efficiency
  sourceUrl: https://www.iea.org/topics/energy-efficiency
  observedAt: 2026-09-29
  explanation: The International Energy Agency tracks energy efficiency in
    buildings, transport, and industry. Providing the same service with less
    energy raises productive efficiency and can reduce both resource pressure
    and emissions.
misconceptions:
  - Producing more does not establish allocative efficiency if society places
    little value on the extra output.
  - An efficient outcome can be very unequal, so efficiency and equity need
    separate judgements.
examApplication:
  - State whether the analysis concerns productive or allocative efficiency.
  - Evaluation should check resource waste, social preferences, and distribution
    rather than output alone.
summary:
  - Productive efficiency concerns avoiding input waste.
  - Allocative efficiency concerns the preferred output mix.
  - Efficiency and equity are distinct criteria.
---
Productive efficiency means that, given existing technology and resources, more of one output cannot be produced without reducing another. It asks whether resources are fully employed and whether output is made at low cost.

Allocative efficiency adds a demand-side test: the combination of output should reflect the relative value society places on different goods and services. An economy may operate on its production possibility frontier and still produce the wrong mix.
