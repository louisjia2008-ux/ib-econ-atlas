Separate the price level from its rate of change.  deflation raises the purchasing power of a fixed nominal amount.  real debt and delayed spending may weaken demand further.

### Original scenario and reasoning

Illustrative example: inflation falling from 8% to 3% still means prices rise; moving from 3% to -1% is deflation. Fixed repayments may become harder when wages and incomes fall. Productivity-driven price falls differ from demand-collapse deflation.

A lower inflation rate does not mean a lower price level.

Define and measure objectives before analysing causes, stakeholders and conflicts.
