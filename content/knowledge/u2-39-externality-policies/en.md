### Original scenario and reasoning

Illustrative example: an emissions tax fixes the price per emission unit, while permits fix the allowed total quantity. Taxes need information about harm and abatement response; permits need monitoring, a sound cap and reliable trading. Both may pass some costs to consumers.

Correction need not eliminate all pollution; the optimum is not always zero emissions.

Identify production/consumption and positive/negative effects, and label private equilibrium and the social optimum.
