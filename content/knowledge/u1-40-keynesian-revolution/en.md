  below full employment, establishing a framework for fiscal and monetary

definition: Keynesian economics holds that expenditure, expectations, and

  self-correction is slow.
causalChain:
  - Pessimistic expectations reduce consumption or investment
  - Lower aggregate demand causes firms to cut output and jobs
  - Falling income further reduces expenditure
  - Countercyclical fiscal or monetary policy seeks to interrupt the contraction

The Great Depression challenged the classical expectation of rapid return to full employment. Keynes emphasised that business investment depends on uncertain expectations and that lower household income reduces consumption, allowing deficient demand to reinforce itself.

The argument helped establish macroeconomics and stabilisation policy. Government budgets, automatic stabilisers, and central-bank action can moderate a cycle, while timing, financing, inflation risk, and long-run supply still require evaluation.
