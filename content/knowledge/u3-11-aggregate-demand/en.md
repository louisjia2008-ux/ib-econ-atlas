### Original scenario and reasoning

Illustrative example: a lower price level can raise the real value of money balances and improve export price competitiveness, increasing quantity of aggregate demand. The axes are real GDP and the general price level, not one product's price and quantity.

AD's downward slope cannot be explained solely by substitution between individual goods.
