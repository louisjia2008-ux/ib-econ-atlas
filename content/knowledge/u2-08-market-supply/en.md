Some inputs are fixed in the short run.  firms adjust variable inputs.  in the long run capacity and market entry can change.

### Original scenario and reasoning

Illustrative example: two firms supply 30 and 20 units at the same price, yielding market supply of 50. Buildings may be fixed for a month but adjustable over a year, so a short-run limit need not be permanent.

Short and long run are defined by input flexibility, not universal calendar durations.

Separate own price, costs and time when explaining supply.
