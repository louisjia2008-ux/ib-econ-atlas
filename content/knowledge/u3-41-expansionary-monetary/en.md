Lower interest rates or ease credit conditions.  interest-sensitive spending increases and aggregate demand shifts right.  a negative output gap may narrow.

### Original scenario and reasoning

Illustrative example: with idle equipment, cheaper finance restores some investment, raising output and employment. Demand may respond weakly if banks restrict lending or households repay debt. Near capacity, price pressure is greater.

A rightward AD shift does not automatically increase potential output.

Trace rates through spending to AD, stating exchange-rate arrangements, lags and limitations.
