Energy costs or wages relative to productivity rise.  SRAS shifts left.  prices and unemployment may rise together.

### Original scenario and reasoning

Illustrative example: dearer transport energy affects many industries. Monetary tightening can restrain second-round demand and expectations but cannot directly produce energy and may deepen output losses. Distinguish the initial shock, expectations and continuing wage-price interactions.

Higher nominal wages need not increase unit labour cost if offset by productivity gains.

Define and measure objectives before analysing causes, stakeholders and conflicts.
