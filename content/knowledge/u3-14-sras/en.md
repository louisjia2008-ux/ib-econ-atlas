Output prices rise relative to temporarily fixed input prices.  production margins improve.  firms expand output along SRAS.

### Original scenario and reasoning

Illustrative example: dearer energy shifts SRAS left, raising the price level and reducing real output with unchanged AD. If output and all input prices rise proportionately, the original short-run real-margin incentive cannot simply be assumed.

A leftward SRAS shift is a cost shock, not a rightward AD shift.

Label price level and real output; distinguish AD, SRAS, LRAS and model assumptions.
