### Original scenario and reasoning

Illustrative example: a 6% nominal lending rate and 2% expected inflation imply an approximately 4% real rate. If bank risk premiums rise, lending rates need not fall alongside policy rates. Money is not income, and new lending does not directly equal new output.

Use expected inflation when analysing ex-ante real rates.

Trace rates through spending to AD, stating exchange-rate arrangements, lags and limitations.
