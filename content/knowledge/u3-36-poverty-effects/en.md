### Original scenario and reasoning

Illustrative example: malnutrition harms learning and adult work capacity; public health and school meals may interrupt the cycle. Assess income gaps by distinguishing opportunity inequality, incentives and rent extraction rather than treating all differences alike.

Efficiency and equity need not conflict under every policy.

Distinguish income, wealth, poverty and inequality; evaluate targeting, incentives and equity criteria.
