---
title: Factors of production
takeaway: Production combines land, labour, capital, and entrepreneurship; both
  the quantity and quality of each factor constrain output.
definition: "Factors of production are the inputs used to create goods and
  services: land (natural resources), labour (human effort and skill), capital
  (produced means of production), and entrepreneurship (organisation,
  innovation, and risk-bearing)."
causalChain:
  - Natural resources provide materials and location
  - Labour contributes time, skill, and judgement
  - Capital raises the productive capacity of labour and resources
  - Entrepreneurship combines inputs under uncertainty
realWorldExample:
  title: Workforce skills affect productive capacity
  sourceUrl: https://www.ilo.org/topics-and-sectors
  observedAt: 2026-09-29
  explanation: International Labour Organization work on skills and employment
    demonstrates how education, training, and working conditions influence the
    quality of labour, not merely the number of workers.
misconceptions:
  - Capital in this classification means machinery, buildings, and tools rather
    than money itself.
  - Entrepreneurship is a function—organising, innovating, and taking risk—not
    the size of a business.
examApplication:
  - Identify concrete inputs in the case rather than only listing four labels.
  - When explaining growth, distinguish a larger quantity of a factor from
    higher factor productivity.
summary:
  - Land includes natural resources.
  - Labour includes human time, effort, and skill.
  - Capital and entrepreneurship organise and raise the productivity of other
    inputs.
---
Every production process requires inputs. Land includes location, minerals, water, and ecosystem services; labour includes human time, effort, and knowledge; capital consists of tools and facilities made for further production; entrepreneurship identifies opportunities and organises the other factors.

The classification also helps explain income and growth. Wages are associated with labour, rent with land, returns with capital, and profit with bearing entrepreneurial uncertainty. A real input can contain characteristics of more than one category.
