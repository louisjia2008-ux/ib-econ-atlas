### Original scenario and reasoning

Hypothetical scenario: machinery imports supporting future exports differ from short-term foreign-currency borrowing funding consumption. Long-term direct investment is generally more stable than easily reversed short-term finance.

Deficits are not automatically bad, nor surpluses always good; compare size, maturity, currency and structure.

Declare the financial-account sign and reserve treatment; distinguish trade from current balances and accounting identities from economic causation.
