Compare export and import prices.  divide indices with the same base.  add quantities and costs to explain receipts.

### Original scenario and reasoning

Hypothetical scenario: export price index ninety and import price index 120 give terms of trade 75. This is not a trade balance of −25 and does not alone determine export receipts.

Do not confuse terms of trade with an exchange rate or exports divided by imports.

Explain reinforcing barriers, identify binding constraints and avoid deterministic claims about countries or groups.
