---
title: Interdependence
takeaway: Interdependence means that one agent's choice affects others through
  trade, income, prices, the environment, or institutions.
definition: Interdependence is the two-way reliance among households, firms,
  governments, countries, and the natural environment created through
  production, exchange, finance, policy, and ecosystems.
causalChain:
  - Agents become connected through markets or institutions
  - Demand, supply, or rules change for one participant
  - Prices, incomes, or resource flows transmit the shock
  - Other participants respond and may create feedback
realWorldExample:
  title: Global trade networks transmit shocks
  sourceUrl: https://www.wto.org/english/res_e/statis_e/statis_e.htm
  observedAt: 2026-09-29
  explanation: World Trade Organization statistics track cross-border flows of
    intermediate and final goods. A change in demand or shipping conditions in
    one economy can influence orders, prices, and employment across many
    production locations.
misconceptions:
  - Interdependence does not imply equal power; dependence may be strongly
    asymmetric.
  - Connections can diversify risk while also spreading and amplifying a shock.
examApplication:
  - Trace at least one two-way effect between identifiable agents.
  - When evaluating integration, consider benefits, vulnerabilities, and
    feedback effects together.
summary:
  - Economic agents are connected through several kinds of flow.
  - Shocks can travel across markets and places.
  - Interdependence creates both gains and risks.
---
Modern production frequently spans several firms and places, while income connects producers to consumers. Government policy, financial conditions, and environmental change also cross the boundary of a single market.

Analysis should identify transmission channels and feedback. Higher costs in one location may raise prices elsewhere; the resulting fall in demand can then return to the original producer. Networks support specialisation and risk sharing but can also spread a local disturbance.
