### Original scenario and reasoning

Illustrative example: tea and coffee are substitutes, so dearer tea shifts coffee demand right. Coffee machines and coffee are complements, so dearer machines may shift coffee demand left. Always name the market being analysed.

Higher income can shift demand for an inferior good left, so income increases do not always shift demand right.

Distinguish movements along demand from shifts and state the ceteris paribus assumptions.
