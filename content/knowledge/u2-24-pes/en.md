### Original scenario and reasoning

Illustrative example: price rises 10% and quantity supplied rises 5%, so PES=0.5: inelastic. Completely fixed immediate output gives a vertical supply curve, but not all short-run supply is perfectly inelastic.

PES measures quantity supplied response, not a demand change or a supply shift.

Use spare capacity and adjustment time to explain supply responsiveness.
