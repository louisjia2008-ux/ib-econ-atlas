### Original scenario and reasoning

Hypothetical scenario: equal HDI can coexist with different IHDI when income, health and education are distributed differently. Similar longevity and well-being with different environmental pressure can yield different HPI. Use the same methodology edition for comparisons.

GII is not simply a ratio of male and female HDI; HPI is neither HDI nor a subjective-happiness-only ranking.

State dimensions, units, years and average-value limits; compare using multiple indicators rather than rankings alone.
