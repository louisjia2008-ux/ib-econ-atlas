### Original scenario and reasoning

Hypothetical scenario: lending 100 adds a deposit of 100 but also a debt of 100, so it does not add 100 to the borrower’s net wealth. Base and broad money differ; reserves do not guarantee unlimited mechanical expansion.

New deposits are not new output, and money creation does not make banks unconstrained.
