### Original scenario and reasoning

Illustrative example: public vacancy platforms improve information and relocation support aids regional job search, but housing costs and caring duties may still restrict mobility. Weaker protections may lower some hiring costs while increasing insecurity; lower wages alone are not a complete evaluation.

Training does not directly solve all cyclical unemployment caused by weak demand.

Explain productivity and potential-output channels, distinguish short-run AD from long-run AS and evaluate opportunity costs.
