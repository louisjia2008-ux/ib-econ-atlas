### Original scenario and reasoning

Hypothetical scenario: tariff-supported car production in a small market may lack scale. Export support tied to performance and logistics can preserve competition but exposes firms to world-demand volatility.

These are not mutually exclusive labels; instruments, exit rules and sector conditions matter.
