Users can benefit without payment.  voluntary contributions are insufficient.  private financing may fail to cover production costs.

### Original scenario and reasoning

Illustrative example: neighbourhood lighting benefits every household, including non-payers. Even if combined benefit exceeds installation cost, voluntary charging may fail. Taxes or community rules can coordinate financing without guaranteeing spending efficiency.

Simply raising the price cannot solve free riding when non-payers still receive benefits.

Classify rivalry and excludability and explain financing rather than merely stating government provision.
