### Original scenario and reasoning

Hypothetical scenario: a world price below autarky increases domestic purchases and reduces home output; imports fill the gap. Consumers gain and producers lose. Numerical trade-volume, expenditure and surplus calculations from diagrams are HL.

A small economy takes the world price; imports are not total domestic consumption.

State small-country, homogeneous-good and cost assumptions; compare welfare and distribution. For HL calculations, start with opportunity costs.
