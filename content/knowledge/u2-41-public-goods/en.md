An additional user does not consume the same service away.  exclusion is difficult or costly.  individual use charges struggle to finance provision.

### Original scenario and reasoning

Illustrative example: national defence generally protects one more resident without reducing protection for others and cannot easily exclude non-payers. A congested road is rival at peak times, so it cannot always be treated as a pure public good.

Public provision is not the same as a public good; a school can still exclude users.

Classify rivalry and excludability and explain financing rather than merely stating government provision.
