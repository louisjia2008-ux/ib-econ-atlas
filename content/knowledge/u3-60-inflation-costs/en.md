### Original scenario and reasoning

Hypothetical scenario: unexpected inflation reduces fixed nominal debt’s real burden, potentially helping debtors and hurting creditors. Wage adjustment affects households. Demand restraint against cost-push inflation may raise unemployment, requiring a cost comparison.

Inflation does not affect everyone equally; low predictable inflation differs from high volatile inflation.
