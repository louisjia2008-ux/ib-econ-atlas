### Original scenario and reasoning

Illustrative example: rapid credit and consumption growth near full employment meets limited production response, raising price pressure. With substantial spare resources, the same demand rise may generate more real growth and less inflation.

A price rise in one product does not necessarily imply economy-wide demand-pull inflation.

Define and measure objectives before analysing causes, stakeholders and conflicts.
