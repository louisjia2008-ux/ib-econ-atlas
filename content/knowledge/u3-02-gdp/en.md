### Original scenario and reasoning

Illustrative example: grain sells for 20, flour for 35 and final bread for 60. Value added is 20+15+25=60, not sales summed to 115. Buying an existing house is not new current output, although current brokerage services can count.

GDP follows domestic production, not the nationality of a firm's owners.
