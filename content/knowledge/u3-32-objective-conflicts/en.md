### Original scenario and reasoning

Illustrative example: fiscal expansion can raise employment in a negative gap but more readily raise prices at capacity. Education spending increases the short-run deficit yet may expand the future tax base if it improves productivity. One improved objective alone does not establish success.

Relationships between objectives are not fixed; state the conditions in evaluation.

Define and measure objectives before analysing causes, stakeholders and conflicts.
