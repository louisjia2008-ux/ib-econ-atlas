### Original scenario and reasoning

Illustrative example: means-tested support improves targeting but may miss households without records; tapering benefits can reduce benefit cliffs. Universal schooling reaches more people, but unequal local quality means nominally free provision need not equalise opportunity.

A larger transfer does not necessarily reduce real poverty by the same proportion.

Distinguish income, wealth, poverty and inequality; evaluate targeting, incentives and equity criteria.
