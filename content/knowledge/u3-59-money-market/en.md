### Original scenario and reasoning

Hypothetical scenario: holding income and prices constant, a rightward money-supply shift lowers equilibrium interest. Actual central banks may target a policy rate directly; the schematic is not every system’s daily operating process.

The axes are nominal interest and money quantity, not a good’s price and output.
