A disequilibrium price makes trading plans incompatible.  buyers compete or sellers accumulate stock.  changing price adjusts quantities on both sides.

### Original scenario and reasoning

Illustrative example: Qd=90 and Qs=50 imply a shortage of 40. A price rise lowers quantity demanded and increases quantity supplied along the curves. Controls can prevent adjustment, so shortages need not disappear immediately.

The size of a shortage is Qd minus Qs, not quantity demanded itself.

Identify the changed determinant before comparing prices, quantities and stakeholders.
