Larger markets support specialisation.  greater scale can reduce average costs.  competition can restrain prices and encourage innovation.

### Original scenario and reasoning

Hypothetical scenario: a medicine producer spreads research costs across a larger market. Consumers may obtain cheaper medicines while workers in less efficient firms face retraining.

Aggregate gains do not mean everyone gains; adjustment costs, environmental effects and dependence require separate evaluation.

State small-country, homogeneous-good and cost assumptions; compare welfare and distribution. For HL calculations, start with opportunity costs.
