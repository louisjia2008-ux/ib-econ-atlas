Import competition reallocates resources.  some firms and workers bear adjustment costs.  retraining and social protection affect the distribution of net gains.

### Original scenario and reasoning

Hypothetical scenario: cheaper imports lower household spending but close a remote region's only factory. Reallocation takes time; national net gains do not erase local losses.

Treating every import as a loss, or all trade as beneficial to everyone, omits stakeholders.

State small-country, homogeneous-good and cost assumptions; compare welfare and distribution. For HL calculations, start with opportunity costs.
