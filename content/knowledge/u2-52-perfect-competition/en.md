A firm cannot influence market price.  it chooses output at MR=MC.  long-run entry and exit remove supernormal economic profit.

### Original scenario and reasoning

Illustrative example: supernormal profit attracts entry, expanding market supply and reducing price until P=min ATC. The long-run model achieves productive efficiency and P=MC allocative efficiency, but homogeneity and information assumptions may fail in practice.

The firm's horizontal demand curve differs from the downward-sloping demand curve of the whole market.

Separate firm and market diagrams, identify MR=MC output and read price from demand.
