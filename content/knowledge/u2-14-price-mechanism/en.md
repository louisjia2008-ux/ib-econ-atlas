### Original scenario and reasoning

Illustrative example: rain damages vegetables and prices rise, encouraging other farms to deliver more and buyers to economise. Ability to pay affects access, so efficient rationing and equitable distribution are distinct criteria.

A high price reflects relative market scarcity, not a direct measure of urgency of human need.

Identify the changed determinant before comparing prices, quantities and stakeholders.
