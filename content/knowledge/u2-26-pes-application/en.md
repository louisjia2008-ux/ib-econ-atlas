Demand stimulus shifts demand right.  capacity constrains supply response.  more inelastic supply normally creates a larger price response.

### Original scenario and reasoning

Illustrative example: price rises from 20 to 22 and supply from 100 to 105. Using initial values, PES=(5/100)/(2/20)=0.5. With a fixed housing stock, a rent subsidy can raise rents in the short run rather than add many homes.

A demand subsidy does not automatically remove supply-side constraints.

Use spare capacity and adjustment time to explain supply responsiveness.
