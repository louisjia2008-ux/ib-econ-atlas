### Original scenario and reasoning

Illustrative example: when demand and supply both shift right, equilibrium quantity rises but price may rise, fall or stay unchanged. Directions alone cannot determine price. With demand right and supply left, price rises but quantity is ambiguous.

Preserve ambiguity under simultaneous shifts rather than silently assuming equal magnitudes.

Identify the changed determinant before comparing prices, quantities and stakeholders.
