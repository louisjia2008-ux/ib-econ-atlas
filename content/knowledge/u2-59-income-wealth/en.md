### Original scenario and reasoning

Illustrative example: a household earns 300,000 yearly, owns assets of two million and owes 800,000: net wealth is 1.2 million. Income and wealth can diverge, as for a retired asset owner. A single measure may misidentify policy beneficiaries.

Wealth is assets minus liabilities, not assets plus the year's income counted again.
