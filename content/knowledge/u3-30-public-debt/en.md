Deficits normally increase financing needs.  debt stocks generate interest costs.  growth and primary balances jointly affect the debt-to-GDP ratio.

### Original scenario and reasoning

Illustrative example: debt of 600 and GDP 1000 give a 60% ratio. Debt rises to 630 but GDP to 1100, lowering the ratio to about 57.3%. There is no universal safe threshold; use of borrowing, maturity and credibility matter.

More government debt does not necessarily imply a higher debt-to-GDP ratio.

Define and measure objectives before analysing causes, stakeholders and conflicts.
