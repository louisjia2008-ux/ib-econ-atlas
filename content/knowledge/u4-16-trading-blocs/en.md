Members lower internal barriers.  free trade areas need rules of origin.  a common external tariff creates a customs union.

### Original scenario and reasoning

Hypothetical scenario: A and B trade duty-free but tax C differently. This is a free trade area; rules of origin limit tariff circumvention through the lowest-tariff member.

Internal free trade alone does not imply a common external tariff or currency.

Distinguish levels of integration; compare trade creation, diversion, policy autonomy and adjustment mechanisms.
