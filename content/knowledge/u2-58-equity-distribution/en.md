### Original scenario and reasoning

Illustrative example: two households want education equally but one cannot afford it. Market demand reflects willingness and ability to pay, not every person's need. State the equity criterion rather than automatically labelling all differences unfair.

Efficiency and equity can conflict, but targeted policy may improve both.
