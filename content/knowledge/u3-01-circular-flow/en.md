Households supply factors and receive income.  income funds consumption or leakages.  injections support firms' sales and the next round of income.

### Original scenario and reasoning

Illustrative example: households supply labour, receive wages and buy firms' output. S, T and M are leakages; I, G and X are injections. Simple steady equilibrium requires planned injections to equal planned leakages, not each individual item to equal its counterpart.

Saving is not money that vanishes; finance can channel funds into investment without making planned saving and investment automatically identical.

State indicators, formulas, units and limits; distinguish nominal, real, aggregate and per-capita measures.
