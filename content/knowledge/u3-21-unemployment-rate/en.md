Distinguish employment, unemployment and inactivity.  calculate using the right denominator.  participation changes can alter the rate without an equivalent employment change.

### Original scenario and reasoning

Illustrative example: 900 employed, 100 unemployed and 1250 working-age people imply unemployment 10% and participation 80%. Discouraged workers leaving job search can lower unemployment without raising employment. Underemployment and informal work also matter.

The unemployment-rate denominator is neither total population nor employed people alone.

Define and measure objectives before analysing causes, stakeholders and conflicts.
