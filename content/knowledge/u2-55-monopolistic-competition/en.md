Differentiation gives downward-sloping firm demand.  supernormal profit attracts close substitutes.  demand weakens until normal profit.

### Original scenario and reasoning

Illustrative example: cafes differentiate through atmosphere and branding. At long-run normal profit, demand is tangent to ATC, normally above its minimum, implying excess capacity. Consumers may value variety, so a cost diagram alone does not capture all welfare.

Normal profit does not imply perfect competition; P may still exceed MC.

Separate firm and market diagrams, identify MR=MC output and read price from demand.
