### Original scenario and reasoning

Illustrative example: two airlines each earn eight at high prices. One cutting price alone earns 12 while the other earns two; both cutting earns four each. Cutting is each firm's dominant strategy, giving (4,4) despite higher joint gains at (8,8). The payoffs are original teaching assumptions.

Joint gains do not ensure collusion is stable or beneficial to consumers.

Separate firm and market diagrams, identify MR=MC output and read price from demand.
