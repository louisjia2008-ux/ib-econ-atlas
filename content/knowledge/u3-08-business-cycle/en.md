### Original scenario and reasoning

Illustrative example: real output moves from 100 to 104, then 102 and 106. The fall to 102 is a contraction despite output remaining above its initial level. Two negative quarters is a common shorthand, not every agency's sole recession criterion.

Slower growth can remain positive and is not necessarily falling real output.

State indicators, formulas, units and limits; distinguish nominal, real, aggregate and per-capita measures.
