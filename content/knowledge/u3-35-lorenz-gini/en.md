Rank incomes and cumulate shares.  compare with perfect equality.  a larger area gap normally indicates more inequality.

### Original scenario and reasoning

Illustrative example: with both axes from zero to one, equality has area 0.5. If the area under Lorenz is 0.3, Gini=(0.5−0.3)/0.5=0.4. Crossing Lorenz curves cannot be ranked by a single local distance.

An income Gini is not a wealth Gini and does not directly count absolute poverty.

Distinguish income, wealth, poverty and inequality; evaluate targeting, incentives and equity criteria.
