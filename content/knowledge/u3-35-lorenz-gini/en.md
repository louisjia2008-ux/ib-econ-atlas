### Original scenario and reasoning

Illustrative example: rank the population from poorest to richest. A Lorenz curve further below equality normally indicates greater inequality. Gini closer to zero means more equality and closer to one more inequality; crossing curves cannot be ranked by one local distance. Constructing the curve from quintile data is HL.

An income Gini is not a wealth Gini and does not directly count absolute poverty.

Distinguish income, wealth, poverty and inequality; evaluate targeting, incentives and equity criteria.
