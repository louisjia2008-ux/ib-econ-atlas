### Original scenario and reasoning

Illustrative example: P=9, AVC=7 and ATC=11 give a loss, but output contributes two per unit toward fixed cost, so short-run operation can be rational. At P=6<AVC production adds to losses. Long-run exit also compares total opportunity costs.

A loss does not necessarily require short-run shutdown; MR=MC does not guarantee positive profit.

Separate firm and market diagrams, identify MR=MC output and read price from demand.
