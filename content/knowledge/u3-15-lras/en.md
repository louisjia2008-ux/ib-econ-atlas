Wages and input prices adjust over time.  short-run real incentives fade.  output returns to the level determined by productive capacity.

### Original scenario and reasoning

Illustrative example: higher AD may briefly push output above Yf. Rising wages then shift SRAS left, restoring Yf at a higher price level. This conclusion depends on wage-price adjustment and competitive assumptions, not an unconditional instant process.

Potential output does not imply zero unemployment; frictional and structural unemployment may remain.
