### Original scenario and reasoning

Illustrative example: one bottled-water brand has many substitutes, whereas drinking water as a whole has fewer. Narrowly defined markets may be more elastic than broad ones. Tax evaluation should distinguish short-run habits from long-run adaptation.

Necessities are often inelastic, but their quantity demanded is not necessarily completely fixed.
