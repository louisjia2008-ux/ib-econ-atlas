Extra output requires inputs.  expansion increases profit when marginal revenue exceeds marginal cost.  stop expanding at the relevant marginal condition.

### Original scenario and reasoning

Illustrative example: output rises from ten to eleven and total cost from 80 to 92, so MC is 12. At a price-taking firm's price of 15, the extra unit adds three to profit. Fixed cost matters for entry but does not directly alter this unit's MC.

Price equal to MC does not prove total profit is positive; price must also be compared with average cost.

Separate own price, costs and time when explaining supply.
