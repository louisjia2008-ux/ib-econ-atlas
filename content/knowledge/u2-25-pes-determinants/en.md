### Original scenario and reasoning

Illustrative example: a printer with idle machines can respond quickly to higher prices, while an orchard needs years to grow mature trees. Long-run input flexibility normally raises elasticity, but land and ecological limits may persist.

More time normally increases PES but does not guarantee it under every constraint.

Use spare capacity and adjustment time to explain supply responsiveness.
