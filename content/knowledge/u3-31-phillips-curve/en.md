### Original scenario and reasoning

Illustrative example: policy temporarily pushes unemployment below the natural rate, then expected inflation rises and SRPC shifts up. An energy shock can raise both inflation and unemployment, so one short-run curve is not a permanently stable policy menu.

Phillips curves use inflation on the vertical axis; AD–AS uses the price level.
