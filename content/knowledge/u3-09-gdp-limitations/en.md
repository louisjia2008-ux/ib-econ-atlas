### Original scenario and reasoning

Illustrative example: pollution cleanup can add to GDP without making pollution beneficial. Unpaid care becoming paid care can raise recorded output without an equivalent increase in services. Cross-country comparisons also depend on informal activity and statistical quality.

GDP's limitations do not make it useless for measuring economic activity.

State indicators, formulas, units and limits; distinguish nominal, real, aggregate and per-capita measures.
