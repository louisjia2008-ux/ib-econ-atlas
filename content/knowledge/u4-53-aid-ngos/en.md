### Original scenario and reasoning

Hypothetical scenario: emergency water grants help after disaster, but expensive donor-country equipment without local repair training may not last. NGOs also need coordination and accountability.

Aid is not all free money; loans create repayment obligations and tied procurement may raise costs.
