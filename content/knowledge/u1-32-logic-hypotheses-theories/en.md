---
title: Logic, hypotheses, theories, and models
takeaway: Economic inquiry derives predictions from logically consistent
  assumptions, represents mechanisms in models, and uses evidence to test and
  improve theory.
definition: A hypothesis is a provisional testable proposition; a theory is a
  coherent system of relationships that explains phenomena; a model is a
  simplified representation of theory; and logic makes inference from premises
  to conclusions internally consistent.
causalChain:
  - Observe a pattern and pose a question
  - Form a hypothesis and proposed mechanism
  - Represent key relationships in a model and derive predictions
  - Use evidence to retain, revise, or replace the theory
realWorldExample:
  title: Policy evaluation connects theory and evidence
  sourceUrl: https://www.oecd.org/en/topics/policy-evaluation.html
  observedAt: 2026-09-29
  explanation: OECD material on policy evaluation stresses clear objectives, a
    theory of change, indicators, and a counterfactual. Evidence can address key
    assumptions only after the logic connecting policy to outcome is explicit.
misconceptions:
  - A theory is not a casual guess; it is a system capable of organising
    explanation and prediction.
  - Valid logic shows that a conclusion follows from premises, not that the
    premises accurately represent reality.
examApplication:
  - Structure an explanation as premises, mechanism, prediction, and evidence.
  - Evaluate internal logical consistency separately from external empirical
    validity.
summary:
  - Logic links premises to conclusions.
  - A hypothesis states a testable proposition.
  - Evidence drives revision of theories and models.
---
An economic explanation often begins with an observed pattern or puzzle. A researcher proposes a mechanism, states assumptions, and builds a model to derive what should be observed if the mechanism operates.

Internal consistency and empirical validity are different tests. A model may be logically sound but rest on assumptions unsuited to a context. It can also fit a pattern in data without identifying the true causal mechanism. Both dimensions need scrutiny.
