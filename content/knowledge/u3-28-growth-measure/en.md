Remove price changes.  compare real output across periods.  use population and capacity to interpret the nature of growth.

### Original scenario and reasoning

Illustrative example: real GDP rises from 800 to 840, giving growth of 5%. With population also growing 5%, real GDP per person is unchanged. Re-employing idle resources can close a gap rather than shift the production frontier.

Using nominal GDP growth without adjusting prices confuses volume with prices.

Define and measure objectives before analysing causes, stakeholders and conflicts.
