Calculate the other good forgone.  compare opportunity costs.  choose a trading ratio between them.

### Original scenario and reasoning

Hypothetical scenario: A can make ten cloth or twenty grain; B six cloth or eighteen grain. One cloth costs two grain in A and three in B. A specialises in cloth; trading at 2.5 grain per cloth can benefit both. Assume mobile domestic resources, full employment and no freight costs.

Even an economy with absolute advantage in both goods can gain from different opportunity costs; adjustment and externalities can weaken gains.

State small-country, homogeneous-good and cost assumptions; compare welfare and distribution. For HL calculations, start with opportunity costs.
