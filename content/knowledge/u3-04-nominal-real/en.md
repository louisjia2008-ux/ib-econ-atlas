### Original scenario and reasoning

Illustrative example: 100 units at price ten become 100 units at price twelve next year. Nominal GDP rises from 1000 to 1200 but real output does not grow. Fixed base-year prices are a teaching simplification; statistical offices may use chain-volume measures.

A nominal GDP fall caused by lower prices does not alone prove a real recession.

State indicators, formulas, units and limits; distinguish nominal, real, aggregate and per-capita measures.
