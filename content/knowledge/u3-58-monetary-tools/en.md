### Original scenario and reasoning

Hypothetical scenario: bond purchases can add settlement balances and, under suitable conditions, lower yields. Lower reserve requirements may ease liquidity without guaranteeing more lending or borrowing. QE often targets longer-term assets.

Do not record central-bank purchases of financial assets as fiscal purchases of goods and services in G.

Trace rates through spending to AD, stating exchange-rate arrangements, lags and limitations.
