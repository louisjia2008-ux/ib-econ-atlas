### Original scenario and reasoning

Illustrative example: a food subsidy helps consumers but requires financing and may also benefit rich households. Compare targeted cash transfers using identification of need, exclusion errors, administrative cost and the ability of supply to expand.

Evaluation is not merely a list of advantages and disadvantages; explain which conditions make an effect matter more.

Label prices, quantities and the policy wedge; evaluate elasticities, public finance and distribution.
