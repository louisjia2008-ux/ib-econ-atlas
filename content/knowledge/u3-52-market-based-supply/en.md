### Original scenario and reasoning

Illustrative example: removing unnecessary entry barriers may strengthen competition, but selling a public monopoly as a private monopoly does not guarantee efficiency or affordability. Tax cuts that fail to induce investment may lose revenue without delivering expected capacity.

Deregulation need not mean removing all safety and environmental rules.
