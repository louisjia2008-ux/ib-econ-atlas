### Original scenario and reasoning

Illustrative example: an energy shortage shifts SRAS left. Expanding AD can limit output losses but raise prices further; contracting AD can limit price pressure but worsen recession. Identify the shock rather than prescribe one policy for every inflation episode.

Distinguish a one-off price-level shock from a continuing inflation process.

Label price level and real output; distinguish AD, SRAS, LRAS and model assumptions.
