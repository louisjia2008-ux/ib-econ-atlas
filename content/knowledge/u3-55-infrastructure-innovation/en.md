### Original scenario and reasoning

Illustrative example: reliable electricity reduces downtime, while research support may generate knowledge spillovers. Underused projects, rent-seeking or poor maintenance can make costs exceed benefits. Research outcomes are uncertain; not every subsidy produces additional innovation.

Higher investment flows do not guarantee capital-service quality or long-run returns.

Explain productivity and potential-output channels, distinguish short-run AD from long-run AS and evaluate opportunity costs.
