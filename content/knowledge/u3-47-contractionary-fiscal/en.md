Cut purchases or raise taxes.  direct demand or disposable income falls.  aggregate demand shifts relatively left and inflation pressure weakens.

### Original scenario and reasoning

Illustrative example: higher income tax during overheating may restrain consumption, but cutting effective training may also damage long-run capacity. In recession, tightening may shrink the tax base, so a unit spending cut need not reduce the deficit by a full unit.

Distinguish direct accounting effects from final macroeconomic effects.

Distinguish purchases/transfers and flows/stocks; evaluate leakages, lags, crowding out and debt.
