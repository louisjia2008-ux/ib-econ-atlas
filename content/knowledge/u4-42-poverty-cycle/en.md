Low income restricts saving.  limited finance constrains capital formation.  low productivity sustains low income.

### Original scenario and reasoning

Hypothetical scenario: a farmer cannot finance irrigation, and low dry-season yields reduce next year's investment capacity. Public irrigation, insurance or suitable finance can interrupt the cycle.

Low saving is only one barrier; credit cannot substitute for viable projects, rights and demand.
