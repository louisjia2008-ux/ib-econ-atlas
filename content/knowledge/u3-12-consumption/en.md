### Original scenario and reasoning

Illustrative example: a tax cut raises disposable income, but households fearing job loss may save the increase. Distinguish the tax reduction from additional consumption rather than assuming every extra unit of income is spent.

Consumption changes from higher income differ from movements along AD caused by a price-level change.
