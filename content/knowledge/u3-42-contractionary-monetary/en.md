Raise policy interest rates.  consumption and investment weaken and aggregate demand is lower than otherwise.  inflation pressure falls but cyclical unemployment may rise.

### Original scenario and reasoning

Illustrative example: higher rates intended to cool excessive demand slow housing investment. If inflation reflects an energy supply disruption, tightening cannot directly restore energy supply and may entail a larger output cost. Fixed-rate contracts can delay the effect.

Reducing inflation usually means slower price increases, not a falling price level.

Trace rates through spending to AD, stating exchange-rate arrangements, lags and limitations.
