Capital or another input is fixed.  variable inputs increase.  crowding reduces marginal product and raises marginal cost.

### Original scenario and reasoning

Illustrative example: one oven with one, two and three workers produces 10, 25 and 35 units. The second worker adds 15, the third adds ten. Total output still rises. Technology must be held constant for this comparison.

Diminishing returns concerns fixed inputs and differs from diseconomies when all inputs vary in the long run.

Separate own price, costs and time when explaining supply.
