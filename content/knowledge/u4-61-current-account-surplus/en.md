### Original scenario and reasoning

Hypothetical scenario: strong exports may support employment and appreciate a floating currency, weakening price competitiveness. Another economy’s surplus may instead reflect recession-driven import compression. Reserve accumulation can alter appreciation.

A surplus is not automatically optimal and does not guarantee appreciation; financial flows and intervention matter.

Declare the financial-account sign and reserve treatment; distinguish trade from current balances and accounting identities from economic causation.
