### Original scenario and reasoning

Illustrative example: median income rises while a household's real income stays unchanged. Its relative position may worsen without necessarily losing the ability to buy basic food. Poverty thresholds depend on prices, household size and public services; an old monetary threshold is not timeless.

Poverty and inequality are related but distinct: everyone can be equally poor.

Distinguish income, wealth, poverty and inequality; evaluate targeting, incentives and equity criteria.
