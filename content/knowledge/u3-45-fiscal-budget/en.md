### Original scenario and reasoning

Illustrative example: revenue of 90 and spending of 100 imply a period deficit of 10, not total debt. A recession-driven larger deficit need not mean a newly announced stimulus. Structural balances try to remove cyclical effects but depend on estimated potential output.

The level of a surplus or deficit alone cannot identify a policy change.

Distinguish purchases/transfers and flows/stocks; evaluate leakages, lags, crowding out and debt.
