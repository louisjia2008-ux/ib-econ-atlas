### Original scenario and reasoning

Hypothetical scenario: an import-dependent economy values stability, but independent rate cuts with mobile capital can pressure a peg. Reserves, credibility and supply adaptability determine feasibility.

No regime is best for every economy; managed floating does not guarantee zero volatility.
