### Original scenario and reasoning

Illustrative example: a large carrier can use specialised maintenance teams to lower cost per delivery, but complex regional management can increase coordination costs. This is long-run analysis with all inputs variable, not diminishing returns from adding workers to a fixed plant.

Economies of scale concerns average cost, not necessarily falling total cost.

Separate firm and market diagrams, identify MR=MC output and read price from demand.
