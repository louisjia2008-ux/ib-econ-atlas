### Original scenario and reasoning

Illustrative example: a factory closure affects workers' households, nearby shops and tax receipts. Training and job-search support may prevent a temporary shock becoming structural, but programme quality, mental health and regional vacancies affect outcomes.

Benefits can cushion household income without automatically restoring all lost output.
