Identify the initial equilibrium.  shift the appropriate curve for the shock.  compare the new equilibrium while stating timing and assumptions.

### Original scenario and reasoning

Illustrative example: stronger export demand shifts AD right, raising prices and output along upward SRAS. If AD and SRAS both shift right, output rises but the price direction depends on magnitudes. A higher price level does not by itself specify annual inflation.

Do not label an AD–AS price-level axis as the inflation rate.

Label price level and real output; distinguish AD, SRAS, LRAS and model assumptions.
