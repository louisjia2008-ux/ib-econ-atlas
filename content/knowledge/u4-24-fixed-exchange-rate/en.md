### Original scenario and reasoning

Hypothetical scenario: the target is 0.5 dollars per home unit and demand falls. Selling dollars to buy home currency supports the target; sustained pressure may require higher rates or policy adjustment.

An official reduction of fixed parity is devaluation, distinct from market depreciation.
