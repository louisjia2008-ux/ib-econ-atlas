Unit costs or productive capacity change.  willingness to produce changes at the same output price.  supply shifts right or left.

### Original scenario and reasoning

Illustrative example: dearer flour raises bread production costs. At each bread price, some previously profitable output becomes unprofitable, shifting bread supply left. Showing this as a demand reduction confuses the two sides of the market.

Changing costs shifts supply; changing the output's own price normally moves along supply.

Separate own price, costs and time when explaining supply.
