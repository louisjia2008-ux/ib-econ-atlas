### Original scenario and reasoning

Illustrative example: under floating exchange rates, lower relative interest may reduce capital inflows and depreciate the currency, supporting net exports while raising import costs. Household cash-flow responses may be slower where fixed-rate mortgages dominate.

A rate cut does not guarantee a fixed exchange-rate or investment response.
