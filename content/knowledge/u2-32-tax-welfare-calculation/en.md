### Original scenario and reasoning

Illustrative example: tax is four and output falls from 100 to 80. With linear curves, DWL is ½×4×20=40 and revenue 4×80=320. If the tax corrects pollution, this no-externality formula cannot directly establish its net welfare effect.

The triangle formula needs assumptions such as linear curves; government revenue is not entirely lost welfare.

Label prices, quantities and the policy wedge; evaluate elasticities, public finance and distribution.
