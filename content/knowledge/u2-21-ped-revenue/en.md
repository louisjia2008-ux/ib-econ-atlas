Price rises while quantity falls.  compare their percentage changes.  if quantity responds proportionately more, the product falls.

### Original scenario and reasoning

Illustrative example: price rises from ten to eleven and quantity falls from 100 to 80. Revenue falls from 1000 to 880. This does not by itself prove profit falls because costs may change too. For finite changes, check the actual P×Q products.

Keep revenue separate from profit, which also deducts costs.
