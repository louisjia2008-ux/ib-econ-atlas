### Original scenario and reasoning

Illustrative example: a seller's costly warranty can signal quality, while a buyer's independent inspection screens it. Mandatory nutrition labels can improve choices if understandable and verified; a mass of unreadable information is not effective information.

A cheap, easily faked signal may fail to distinguish types.

State whether the information problem occurs before or after exchange and evaluate credibility of remedies.
