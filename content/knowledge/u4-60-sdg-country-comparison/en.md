### Original scenario and reasoning

World Bank SH.DYN.MORT data, checked 2026-10-18 and sourced from the UN Inter-agency Group for Child Mortality Estimation: under-five deaths per 1,000 live births were 36.6 in China in 2000 and 6.6 in 2022; India recorded 91.8 and 29.5. Both declined but differed in starting levels, reductions and final levels. This concerns SDG 3 child health, not achievement of all health goals. Source: https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SH.DYN.MORT?locations=CN-IN .

Per 1,000 live births is not a population percentage; national averages hide regional gaps and estimates can be revised.

Match policy to constraints; evaluate implementation, finance, lags, equity and sustainability, then give a conditional judgement.
