Divide total income by population.  adjust purchasing power for cross-country comparison.  add indicators to address average-income limits.

### Original scenario and reasoning

Hypothetical scenario: A and B have equal exchange-rate-converted income, but basic goods are cheaper in A. PPP may show greater purchasing power in A; regional inequality still matters.

PPP is not a tradable market exchange rate; average income is not necessarily a typical household's income.

State dimensions, units, years and average-value limits; compare using multiple indicators rather than rankings alone.
