Recession lowers tax payments and raises some benefits.  disposable income falls less sharply.  consumption and aggregate demand decline less.

### Original scenario and reasoning

Illustrative example: existing unemployment insurance rules automatically pay more benefits as unemployment rises, pushing the budget toward deficit. They cushion rather than eliminate recession; limited coverage, eligibility restrictions or low replacement rates weaken stabilisation. Recovering tax revenues work in reverse during expansion.

Automatic does not mean undesigned or a new stimulus law.

Distinguish purchases/transfers and flows/stocks; evaluate leakages, lags, crowding out and debt.
