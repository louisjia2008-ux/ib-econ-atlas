Fixed cost is spread over more units.  changing marginal returns affect variable inputs.  the relation of MC to the average determines its direction.

### Original scenario and reasoning

Illustrative example: Q=10, TFC=40 and TVC=60 give TC=100, ATC=10, AVC=6 and AFC=4. If the next unit's MC is eight, new ATC=(100+8)/11≈9.82. MC is not TC/Q.

Higher fixed cost raises ATC but does not directly shift MC or AVC.

Separate firm and market diagrams, identify MR=MC output and read price from demand.
