One currency reduces conversion frictions.  one interest rate may not suit all shocks.  mobility and fiscal transfers aid adjustment.

### Original scenario and reasoning

Hypothetical scenario: A overheats while B contracts, so one interest rate cannot perfectly suit both. Labour mobility, price-wage flexibility and fiscal risk sharing affect costs; a common currency does not eliminate credit risk.

Do not list gains without considering asymmetric shocks and autonomy costs.

Distinguish levels of integration; compare trade creation, diversion, policy autonomy and adjustment mechanisms.
