### Original scenario and reasoning

Illustrative example: one more unit yields benefit 12, private cost nine and external pollution cost five, making social cost 14. The unit is privately beneficial but socially harmful. Compare marginal values, not average benefits and costs.

A high price or inequality alone does not establish allocative failure; identify the mechanism producing inefficiency.

Identify production/consumption and positive/negative effects, and label private equilibrium and the social optimum.
