### Original scenario and reasoning

Hypothetical scenario: an economy wants lower rates for jobs, a fixed parity and free capital flows. Persistent outflows expose the difficulty of sustaining all three.

The constraint depends on mobility, risk and credibility; it does not imply identical interest rates everywhere at every moment.
