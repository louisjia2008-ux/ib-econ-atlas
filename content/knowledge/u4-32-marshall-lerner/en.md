Depreciation changes relative prices.  volumes respond to demand elasticities.  the balance improves when volume effects outweigh the import unit-cost effect.

### Original scenario and reasoning

Hypothetical scenario: elasticities 0.7 and 0.6 sum to 1.3, supporting improvement with sufficient supply response and the model's assumptions; improvement need not be immediate.

The condition concerns the trade balance, not all income and transfers; an elasticity is not the quantity percentage change itself.

Declare the financial-account sign and reserve treatment; distinguish trade from current balances and accounting identities from economic causation.
