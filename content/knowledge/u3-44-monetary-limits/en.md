### Original scenario and reasoning

Illustrative example: in recession, firms expecting weak sales may not revive investment after a small cut near the effective lower bound. A credible inflation target can anchor expectations, but independence does not remove accountability or exposure to supply shocks.

The same interest rate affects savers, borrowers and regions differently.
