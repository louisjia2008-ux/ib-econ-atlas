### Original scenario and reasoning

Hypothetical scenario: a coffee cooperative adds processing and ecotourism, potentially adding value and spreading risk. Diversification is limited if both remain exposed to the same climate or visitor market.

More projects do not necessarily diversify risk; social intent does not guarantee operational or impact success.

Match policy to constraints; evaluate implementation, finance, lags, equity and sustainability, then give a conditional judgement.
