Hold production conditions constant.  a higher price increases the return on extra production.  quantity supplied normally rises along the curve.

### Original scenario and reasoning

Illustrative example: cake prices rise from 20 to 25 and a baker works longer to make ten more. With input prices and technology unchanged, this is a movement along supply, not a shift.

Supply is not simply inventory; inventory enters supply when the producer is willing to sell it at the relevant price.

Separate own price, costs and time when explaining supply.
