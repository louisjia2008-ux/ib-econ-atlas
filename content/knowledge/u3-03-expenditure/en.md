Add household, business, government and foreign spending.  subtract imports already included in spending.  exclude financial trades and transfers as new output.

### Original scenario and reasoning

Illustrative example: C=400, I=100, G=150, X=80 and M=60 give GDP=670. Buying shares transfers ownership rather than counting as I. Benefits may enter consumption when spent on new output. Imports are deducted to exclude foreign production.

Imports are deducted because GDP measures domestic output, not because imports are inherently harmful.

State indicators, formulas, units and limits; distinguish nominal, real, aggregate and per-capita measures.
